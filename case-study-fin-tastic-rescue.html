Project description

FIN-tastic Rescue is an interactive animation system designed to visualize water waste in campus toilets by connecting to faucet usage. The project features a digital fish tank display where the water level drops in real time as users wash their hands, creating an immediate visual connection between water consumption and environmental impact. An interactive fish character named "FIN" engages users through dialogue, making water conservation awareness both educational and engaging.

Research & analysis

The question

How can the university cut water waste by raising awareness, rather than by force?

Observation put students at an average of 1.2 litres per 10–12 second handwash — invisible to them, because nothing on the tap says so.

What made it hard
  • Influence without disrupting Effective enough to change behaviour, quiet enough not to be resented
  • Real-time sensing Tracking actual usage accurately, live at the tap
  • A metaphor that reads instantly A fish tank people understand without a caption
  • Teaching vs. engaging Getting the message across without lecturing
What we decided
  1. Awareness over enforcement — no forced shut-offs. Show people the cost and let them choose.
  2. Make the abstract visible — a draining tank turns litres into something you watch happen.
  3. A character, not a warning — FIN carries the message so it lands as a moment, not a scolding.
